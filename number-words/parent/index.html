How each phase works, what it teaches, and how answers are checked.

How each phase works

For the parent
  1. Phase 1 (0–10): Learn the word, say it and spell it aloud, Look-Cover-Write-Check, practice with hints, and a 10-word spelling test (mastery is 9 or more).
  2. Phases 2, 3 and 4 work like a math lesson: a Phase Menu with Learn (an example, then your turn), Practice (Practice Together, On My Own, and practice for one skill), a test, My Results, and a Parent Guide for that phase.
  3. Help in Phases 2 and 3: every Learn and practice question has Check Answer, a Hint, and Show Me. A hint gives a clue, never the whole answer. Show Me gives the answer and explains it, and your child can go on. Answers seen with Show Me are counted as completed with help, never as correct on their own and never toward mastery.
  4. Tests have no hints, no Show Me and no feedback until they are finished. My Results then shows each answer with an explanation, results by skill (number recognition and spelling), and practice for missed skills.
  5. Saved progress stays in this browser. Each phase is saved and reset on its own; resetting one phase does not change the others or the math lessons.

Spelling and checking answers

For the parent

  • Capital letters and spaces before or after an answer do not matter.
  • Between the tens and the ones, a hyphen or a space is accepted: forty-two or forty two.
  • Every letter must be right. A missing part (forty for 42) or a different number is not accepted.
  • Number answers are typed with digits. An empty answer is never counted as 0.
