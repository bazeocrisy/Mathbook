How each phase works

For the parent
  1. Learn: see the numeral, the word, and how it is spelled.
  2. Say: say the word, then spell it aloud letter by letter.
  3. Look, Cover, Write, Check: study the word, cover it, write it from memory, and compare.
  4. Practice: varied spelling activities with hints and second tries.
  5. Test: 10 typed spellings, no hints. Mastery is 9 or more correct.
