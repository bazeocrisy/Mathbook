## 2. Goals and prerequisites

Goal: *I can show what I learned in Unit 2: place value, rounding, estimating, adding and subtracting with many strategies, and comparing numbers.*
Prerequisites: lessons 2-1 to 2-15. The review is used after the unit; results tell the parent which lesson to revisit.

## 3. Vocabulary (child-friendly)

- **Decompose** — break a number into parts. 846 = 800 + 40 + 6.
- **Estimate** — find an answer that is close, not exact.
- **Round** — change a number to a nearby ten or hundred.
- **Compatible number** — a number close to the real one that is easy to use in your head.
- **Expanded form** — a number written as the sum of the values of its digits. 3,257 = 3,000 + 200 + 50 + 7.

## 10. Common mistakes and coaching

- 2-1: writing 4,071 as 471 or 4,710 (forgetting the 0 hundreds).
- 2-2: rounding halfway down (865 → 860); rounding to the nearest 100 when asked for 10 (594 → 600).
- 2-3: exact answer instead of an estimate; rounding to the wrong place.
- 2-5: thinking odd + odd = odd.
- 2-7/2-11: subtracting in the wrong order or taking away the wrong part.
- 2-8: adding to both addends (350 + 287) instead of adding to one and taking from the other.
- 2-12: choosing equations that are true but answer a different question (C/D distractors).
- 2-13: comparing first digits of different-length numbers.
- 2-14/2-15: forgetting regrouped 1s; lining up on the left.
