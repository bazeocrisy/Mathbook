**Important observation:** every subtraction on these pages (Learn, Work Together, items 1–12, and Unit Review item 9) needs **no regrouping** — every top digit is at least the bottom digit. This spec keeps 2-15 regroup-free to match the book (see §14).

## 2. Goals and prerequisites

Goal: *I can subtract multi-digit numbers with an algorithm: line up the places, subtract the ones, then the tens, hundreds, thousands (and ten thousands).*

- Write the numbers vertically with the ones lined up.
- Subtract place by place from right to left; write a 0 when a place subtracts to 0.
- Subtract numbers with different numbers of digits (3, 4, 5 digits).
- Check a difference with addition.
- Find missing digits by reasoning about each place.
- Solve "how many are left," "find the other part," and "compare two increases" word problems.

Prerequisites: 2-1 (place value), 2-11 (fluently subtract within 1,000), 2-14 (addition algorithm).

## 3. Vocabulary

- **Difference** — the answer to a subtraction problem.
- **Algorithm** — steps you do the same way every time.
- **Vertical** — written in a column, one number under the other.
- **Line up** — put ones under ones, tens under tens, and so on.
- **Increase** — how much bigger a number got.
- Review: place value, digit.

## 4. Methods taught in Learn

1. **Subtraction algorithm by place value** (book Steps 1–4). Write the numbers vertically, ones under ones. Subtract the ones, then the tens, hundreds, thousands. Book example: 8,386 − 7,185: 6 − 5 = 1; 8 tens − 8 tens = 0; 3 hundreds − 1 hundred = 2 hundreds; 8 thousands − 7 thousands = 1 thousand → 1,201. Reasoning: each place is subtracted separately, so lining up keeps like units together.
2. **Connect to the addition algorithm** ("Structure"): same layout, same right-to-left order; the difference + the number subtracted = the starting number (check).
3. Implicit in On My Own: different lengths (line up the ones), 5-digit numbers, missing digits, word problems (take away, part-whole, compare increases).

## 5. Guided sequence (Learn steps)

Five steps. Learn demo numbers (not reused): 9,486 − 6,253; 5,867 − 742; 28,975 − 3,510; 3,856 − 1,343.

### Step 1 · Subtract place by place
Example slides (`vcalcHTML` static, focus column highlighted — see 2-14 §13 A):
1. "A truck weighs 9,486 pounds. A van weighs 6,253 pounds. How much heavier is the truck? 9,486 − 6,253." Write them vertically, ones under ones.
2. Ones: 6 − 3 = **3**.
3. Tens: 8 tens − 5 tens = **3** tens.
4. Hundreds: 4 hundreds − 2 hundreds = **2** hundreds.
5. Thousands: 9 thousands − 6 thousands = **3** thousands. "The truck is **3,233** pounds heavier."

Your Turn (`vcalc`, op −): 4-digit − 4-digit, every column top digit ≥ bottom digit, top thousands > bottom thousands, at most one zero in the difference. Hint: "Start with the ones. Subtract the bottom digit from the top digit in each place."

### Step 2 · Line up different sizes (and zeros)
Example slides:
1. "5,867 − 742. Line up the **ones**: 2 goes under 7, not under 5." Show wrong (left-aligned, crossed out) vs right.
2. Work it: 7 − 2 = 5; 6 − 4 = 2; 8 − 7 = 1; 5 − nothing = 5 → **5,125**. "If the bottom has no digit, bring the top digit down."
3. "Zeros: 6,438 − 2,418: 8 − 8 = **0**, 3 − 1 = 2, 4 − 4 = **0**, 6 − 2 = 4 → **4,020**. Write the 0s — they hold the places."
4. "Big numbers work the same: 28,975 − 3,510 → 5, 6, 4, 5, 2 → **25,465**."

Your Turn (`vcalc`, op −): random from (a) 4-digit − 3-digit, (b) 4-digit − 4-digit with one or two 0s in the difference, (c) 5-digit − 4-digit. All regroup-free. Hint: "Line up the ones first. A place that subtracts to nothing still needs a 0 if a digit comes after it on the left."

### Step 3 · Check with addition
Example slides:
1. "Addition and subtraction undo each other. If 9,486 − 6,253 = 3,233, then 3,233 + 6,253 should be 9,486."
2. vcalc + : 3 + 3 = 6, 3 + 5 = 8, 2 + 2 = 4, 3 + 6 = 9 → 9,486 ✓. "It matches, so the difference is right."
3. "If the check does not match, look for the place that went wrong."

Your Turn (`parts`): generated regroup-free a − b. Part 1 `num`: the difference. Part 2 `choice`: "Which addition checks it?" → "{d} + {b} = {a}" ✓ / "{d} + {a} = {b}" / "{a} + {b} = {d}" / "{d} − {b} = {a}". Hint: "Add the difference to the number you took away."

### Step 4 · Missing digits
Example slides (vcalc with blank cells — §13):
1. Show `3,8□6 − 1,□4□ = □,513`. "Work one place at a time. Start with the ones."
2. Ones: 6 − □ = 3. "6 take away what is 3? **3**."
3. Tens: □ − 4 = 1. "What minus 4 is 1? **5**." (Think 1 + 4.)
4. Hundreds: 8 − □ = 5 → **3**. Thousands: 3 − 1 = □ → **2**.
5. "3,856 − 1,343 = 2,513. Check: 2,513 + 1,343 = 3,856 ✓."

Your Turn (`vcalc` with `blanks`): generate a regroup-free 4-digit − 4-digit; hide exactly 4 digits: one in the top row, two in the bottom row, one in the difference, all in different columns (so each column has exactly one unknown — the answer is unique). Hint: "In each place: top − bottom = difference. Use the two digits you can see to find the missing one."

### Step 5 · Word problems
Example slides:
1. "How many are left? A hike is 1,875 meters. Dev has walked 643 meters. 1,875 − 643 = **1,232** meters left."
2. "Find the other part: A family spent $2,968 on a TV and a stand. The stand cost $415. The TV: 2,968 − 415 = **$2,553**."
3. "Compare increases (table): Plants sold — Year 1: 1,203, Year 2: 3,415, Year 3: 5,638. Year 1 → 2: 3,415 − 1,203 = **2,212**. Year 2 → 3: 5,638 − 3,415 = **2,223**. 2,223 > 2,212, so the increase from Year 2 to Year 3 was greater."

Your Turn (`parts`): generated from two templates: (a) "left" (total − used), (b) "other part" (total − one part). Part 1 `choice`: "Which equation solves it?" ({total} − {part} = ? ✓ / {total} + {part} = ? / {part} − {total} = ?). Part 2 `num`. All regroup-free. Hint: "You know the whole and one part. Subtract to find the other part."

Learn reflection: "Why is it helpful to write numbers vertically when you subtract?" Idea: "The places line up — ones under ones, tens under tens — so you subtract like units, one place at a time."

## 10. Common mistakes and coaching

- Lining up on the left (4,869 − 523 with 5 under 4). Fix: line up the ones first; say "ones under ones."
- Subtracting top from bottom in a column ("5 − 7"). Not needed here (no regrouping), but if the child swaps rows, stop and re-read: top − bottom.
- Dropping a middle 0 (5,040 written as 540). Fix: every place in the answer gets a digit.
- Forgetting to bring down the top digit when the bottom number is shorter (6,957 − 615 → 342). Fix: "nothing to take away — write the top digit."
- Missing digits: subtracting when the unknown is in the top row. Fix: top = bottom + difference.
- Increase problem: comparing the yearly totals instead of the increases. Fix: find both increases first.

## 11. Parent Guide content

- **Goal**: subtract numbers up to 5 digits with the algorithm (no regrouping in this lesson), check with addition, and solve problems.
- **Words**: difference, algorithm, vertical, line up, increase.
- **Demonstrate**: (1) 9,486 − 6,253 on squared paper, one place at a time, right to left (3,233). (2) 5,867 − 742: show the wrong left-aligned version, then the right one (5,125). (3) Check 3,233 + 6,253 = 9,486. (4) The missing-digit puzzle `3,8□6 − 1,□4□ = □,513`.
- **Ask**: "Which place do you start with?" "Are the ones lined up?" "How can you check with addition?" "What plus 4 makes 9?" "What does the question want — what's left, the other part, or which grew more?"
- **Mistakes**: as §10.
- **Checklist**: lines up by place · subtracts right to left and writes 0s · handles 3-, 4-, 5-digit numbers · checks with addition · finds missing digits · solves left / other-part / increase problems.

## 13. New interaction or model needed

Uses **`vcalc`** from 2-14 §13 A with op '−', plus:

- **`blanks` mode** (missing digits): `blanks: { top: ['T'], bottom: ['H', 'O'], result: ['Th'] }` (place keys O, T, H, Th, TTh). Hidden cells render as input boxes inside the rows; all other cells are printed digits; the answer row shows its known digits as printed text. Graded: every blank equals the true digit (all-or-nothing, with per-cell marking in review). Content guarantees one unknown per column and no regrouping, so the answer is unique.
- **"Wrong alignment" static render** for p14/t14 and Learn 2: `vcalcHTML({ ..., misalign: 'left' })` draws the bottom number left-aligned under the top number with a red outline (no inputs).
- **Data table (static)** for p12/t12/Learn 5: a simple 3-column HTML table in the prompt; no new interaction.
- No regroup (borrow) marks are needed for 2-15. If 2-11 needs subtraction-with-regrouping marks, add them there (cross-out digit + small new digit above), and 2-15 can opt in later.
